About Integral Power Solutions

Lighting manufacturer in Nashik, established 2005.

Our story

Integral Power Solutions Private Limited began with a simple vision: to provide reliable, efficient and practical power and lighting solutions for businesses and industries. Since 2005, we have grown through a focus on quality, customer relationships and an understanding of the changing needs of the market.

Over the years we developed our expertise in industrial and commercial LED lighting, helping organisations improve illumination, energy efficiency and lighting performance. We have also expanded into solar energy, where we market and supply solar solutions for the growing demand for cleaner energy.

Rather than simply supplying products, we build long-term relationships by providing the right solution, professional support and consistent service. As the energy and lighting industries evolve, we plan to grow with them and widen our range of lighting and renewable-energy solutions.

Our founders

Integral Power Solutions was founded by Shubhang Vazalwar and Dinesh Patil, who are both directors of the company.

Who we serve

We mainly serve B2B customers: manufacturing facilities, factories, warehouses and logistics companies, infrastructure and outdoor projects, and commercial buildings and offices that need energy-efficient LED lighting, specialised lighting and dependable after-sales support.

Our mission

To deliver high-performance, energy-efficient and sustainable lighting solutions that create long-term value for our customers.
